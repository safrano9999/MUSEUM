## Purpose

Store reusable content formats that work for this account. The Writer uses this library to execute without reinventing structure every time.

A good format is: recognizable, repeatable, flexible, effective, and voice-consistent.

## Formats

### Quick Contrarian Take

**Use case:** When a trend is hot but the account can offer a sharper or more grounded angle.

**Structure:**
- Hook (the contrarian claim)
- Tension or disagreement (why everyone is wrong or missing something)
- Concise justification (the actual reasoning)
- Optional closing line

**Tone:** Sharp, confident, not bloated.

**Why it works:** Gives the account a recognizable point of view without needing a long thread.

**Risks:** Can become empty edge-posting if not grounded in substance.

**Example prompt to Writer:** *Draft 3 concise contrarian variants on this topic. Each should have a distinct hook and clear justification.*

---

### Mini Explainer Thread

**Use case:** When a trend needs framing or interpretation, not just reaction.

**Structure:**
- Strong opening claim
- 3–6 short, specific points
- One synthesizing close

**Tone:** Clear, useful, compact.

**Why it works:** Turns fast-moving noise into lasting value and builds subject authority.

**Risks:** Becomes generic if the angle is weak or the points are obvious.

**Example prompt to Writer:** *Draft a short thread explaining why this trend matters and what most people are missing about it.*

---

### Reply Ladder

**Use case:** When the account should enter an existing conversation rather than post cold.

**Structure:**
- Direct reply hook
- Useful point or reframing
- Optional follow-up reply

**Tone:** Native, conversational, not overproduced.

**Why it works:** Builds visibility through existing attention flows.

**Risks:** Can feel opportunistic if badly targeted or too self-promotional.

**Example prompt to Writer:** *Draft 5 reply options for this conversation, ranging from safe to sharp.*

---

### Series Post

**Use case:** When a topic should become a recurring franchise that builds account memory.

**Structure:**
- Familiar opening pattern (recognizable to returning readers)
- Fresh instance or example
- Recognizable payoff
- Subtle continuity with previous entries

**Tone:** Consistent and branded.

**Why it works:** Builds repeat readership and makes the account feel deliberate.

**Risks:** Gets stale without variation. Requires enough content depth to sustain.

**Example prompt to Writer:** *Draft the next entry in the [series name] series. Use the same recognizable format. Fresh example, same voice.*

---

## Library Rules

- Keep only formats worth repeating
- Retire formats that stop working (move to Archive section below)
- Update examples when stronger versions emerge
- Prefer formats that strengthen identity, not just short-term numbers
