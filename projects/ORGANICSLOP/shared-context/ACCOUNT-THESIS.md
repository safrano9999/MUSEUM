## Good vs. Bad Engagement

**Good engagement:**
- Thoughtful replies from the target audience
- Reposts from relevant accounts
- Saves and bookmarks
- New followers who match the intended profile

**Bad engagement:**
- Empty controversy reactions
- Followers who will never engage again
- Reactions that damage positioning
- Spam or bot interactions

---

## Strategic Filters

Before engaging with any signal, the Strategist asks:

1. Does it fit the niche?
2. Does it strengthen the account identity?
3. Is there an angle we can own?
4. Will it likely attract the right followers?
5. Is this better as a post, thread, reply chain, or series?
6. Is this worth doing more than once?
