# SOCIAL GROWTH AGENTS

A 3-agent OpenClaw team for growing a social media account through niche consistency, signal-aware content selection, and iterative learning loops.

This is **not** a posting bot or trend spammer.
The purpose is to build an account that becomes **more valuable over time**.

---

## What It Does

- Scouts platform trends and surfaces niche-relevant signals
- Selects angles, formats, and topics that strengthen account identity
- Writes platform-native posts, threads, hooks, and replies
- Learns from signal quality and audience reactions over time
- Maintains account coherence across content cycles

---

## Agent Team

| Agent | Name | Emoji | Role |
|-------|------|-------|------|
| Scout | Pulse | 📡 | Trend monitoring, signal detection, example collection |
| Strategist | Forge | 🧠 | Angle selection, positioning, content format decisions |
| Writer | Echo | ✍️ | Posts, threads, hooks, replies, series execution |

---

## Design Principles

**Niche coherence over random reach.** Every content decision must fit the account thesis.
**Quality over volume.** Better execution beats more output.
**Learning loops over one-off posts.** The system improves with each cycle.
**Follower quality over vanity metrics.** Right audience beats big numbers.

---

## Daily Growth Loop

```
Step 1 — Scout    → Trends, signals, examples, momentum, risk notes
Step 2 — Strategy → Topic selection, angle, format, rejection rationale
Step 3 — Write    → Hooks, post drafts, threads, replies, follow-ups
Step 4 — Feedback → Track reactions, update signal log and format library
```

## Shared Context Files

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|

---

## Prerequisites

- OpenClaw installed with LLM API key
- Telegram access (3 bots, 1 supergroup, 3 topics)
- A defined account niche and platform target
- Completed `ACCOUNT-THESIS.md` before first run

---

## Non-Goals

- Spam automation
- Fake engagement or botnet behavior
- Generic content spraying without identity
- Manipulative platform abuse
- Destroying account coherence for short-term numbers

---

## Success Criteria

- The account develops a clear, recognizable niche identity
- Content feels coherent and platform-native across cycles
- The team spots useful trends early, before saturation
- Strategy improves from cycle to cycle based on real signals
- The account attracts the intended audience, not just random reach
